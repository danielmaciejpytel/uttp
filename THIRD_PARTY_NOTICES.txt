== Animation data ==
The motion-matching databases, overlays, traversal and lean tables shipped with UTPP are original
work. They are based on licensed GASPALS 1.2 content by Polygon Hive, then heavily modified,
extended, tuned and blended with the game's own animations for Cyberpunk 2077. The GASPALS content
was used under a purchased license.

== Generated clips (NVIDIA) ==
Some transition, vehicle-exit and melee animation clips were generated locally with NVIDIA Kimodo
and NVIDIA MotionBricks (models licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License and
the NVIDIA Open Model Agreement, respectively). No NVIDIA model is included.

== Content derived from Cyberpunk 2077 ==
Weapon recoil profiles and animation clips, graphs and resource patches built from or for the
animations and resources of Cyberpunk 2077 and its Phantom Liberty expansion (c) CD PROJEKT RED are
derived from game content. They do not contain the game and work only together with a legitimate
copy of it.
